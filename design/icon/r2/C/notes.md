# C. The night window

- **What works:** orange against the night is the strongest contrast of the window drawings. In greyscale Miso sits at about 70% grey against a sky of 16 to 30%, so she holds without the colour. She's bigger than in round 1: 39% of the tile's width (it was about 34%) and 57% of its height up to the ear tips, 74% with the tail. At 64 and 128 px in the Dock you see the cat first. The sky runs from #1B2350 (near the app's own night, #1F2756) to Kavana teal (#2C5C6F) at the sill, and it has two sparkles and three dots, no moon.
- **What doesn't:** the round window is still the biggest shape, and it's the darkest tile in the strip beside Moshe's yellow and Daf's white. It stands out, but it's heavy. An orange cat under stars still leans a little towards Halloween, even with no moon, a navy-to-teal sky rather than black and purple, and the cream frame that keeps her indoors.
- **Small (`icon-small.svg`):** the window fills the tile, the stars go, and Miso is chunkier with taller ears that sit wider apart. At 32 px it's clearly a cat. At 16 px the ears survive as two bumps with a notch between them, and the tail is a one-pixel line. It's much better than the big drawing shrunk, but 16 px is still 16 px.
- **Craft:** it's round 1's one-path cat, scaled up, with the head tilted 3° towards the brightest star and a faint top-to-bottom tint on the fur for the room light (one tone, nearly flat). I tried a smooth neck, but the head turned into jowls on a bottle, so the crisp notch stays. I also tried tabby stripes on the crown, which read as a comb, so they're gone. Drafts are in `drafts/` and the generator is `gen.py` (`python3 gen.py final`).
