# Kavana icon, round 1: the brief

## The brief in five lines

1. **The one idea:** stay with one thing, and a cat keeps you company while you do. A kitten
   grows while you focus and comes home to your café if you stay. Kavana (כוונה) is Hebrew for
   intention.
2. **Audience:** one person, every day: on a phone's first screen beside Instagram and WhatsApp,
   and in the Mac's Dock beside Moshe (a white camel on yellow), Daf (a beech tree on two books,
   on a white tile) and Dvarim.
3. **Feeling:** warm, calm, a little funny, like a cat café. Cosy rather than cute-overload;
   a quiet room, not a game.
4. **Constraints:**
   - Catty: a cat has to be what you see first.
   - Flat or nearly flat. Moshe's camel is the bar: one clean silhouette, at most a lighter tint
     for what's further back and a dot for an eye.
   - No letters or words, Hebrew included (they read as numbers at small sizes).
   - **No spirals or swirls.** A curled-up cat seen from above reads as a spiral. Don't.
   - No clip-art cat head with whiskers and a big smile, no paw print on its own, and no clock.
   - The current icon is a cream ring and dot on teal. It's too bland: don't just put ears on it.
5. **Sizes:** iOS and macOS 1024 down to 16, Android 512 and a maskable version, and the web
   favicon at 32 and 16. It has to read at 16 px.

## Colours

From the app (`web/style.css`, `web/cats.js`).

- **Teal** (the app's tint): `#2C8A7E`, deep `#1F6B61`, light `#5FD0C0`.
- **Café cream** (the wall): `#FDF5E8` to `#F5E4CB`.
- **Miso orange** (the first cat): fur `#EEA45C`, markings `#B96D2A`, ear pink `#F5C4B3`.
- **Ink:** `#2B211A`.
- **Tile:** white (`kit.tile`, which fades to `#F2EFEA`), teal, or cream, whichever your
  concept wants. Say why.

## Principles we judge by

1. **One subject** with a strong silhouette that still reads at 16 px.
2. **One light, from above.** Depth comes from at most a few layers.
3. **At most three tones on the subject.**
4. **From Kavana's world:** the kitten growing in the timer ring, the round window it sits in
   on the Focus tab, the cushion, the café, the night sky at the end of a block. Not from the
   "pomodoro app" category (tomatoes, stopwatches, checkmarks).
5. **Size and position:** the subject fills about 60 to 68% of the tile's height or width, and
   sits on the tile's visual centre, a little above the geometric one (about y = 500).
6. **No unintended readings:** no shapes that look like letters, numbers, a bat, a fox or a devil.
7. **The Dock test:** it must look at home next to Moshe's camel
   (`/srv/dev/moshe-next/frontend/public/icons/icon-512.png`) and Daf's tree
   (`/srv/dev/daf/web/icon-512.png`).

## Twelve concepts

1. **The tail ring:** a sitting cat, seen from the side, whose tail sweeps up and around it into
   the timer ring. The ring Kavana has always had, but now it's a cat.
2. **The window:** the round window from the Focus tab, with a cat sitting in it seen from
   behind, looking out at the sky. **Risk:** a moon or a porthole.
3. **The loaf:** one cat lying in a loaf, eyes shut, perfectly calm. The focus posture.
   **Risk:** a bread roll, a slug.
4. **The ring kitten:** the timer ring as a progress arc, with a small kitten sitting inside it
   where the arc is still open, the way the kitten grows in the app.
5. **The peek:** a cat peeking up over the bottom edge of the tile, paws on the edge, the way
   the cat peeks over a check-in card. **Risk:** only ears and eyes, which turns into a bat.
6. **The cushion:** Miso sitting on her round cushion, a little painted, like Daf's tree.
   **Risk:** too detailed at 16 px.
7. **Night window:** the window at the end of a block, stars in it, a cat's silhouette on the sill.
8. **The cup:** a cat in a café cup. **Risk:** every cat café's logo.
9. **Two cats:** a big one and a kitten side by side. **Risk:** busy.
10. **Sun spot:** a cat asleep in a square of window light on the floor.
11. **The stretch:** a cat mid-stretch, one long line. **Risk:** an animal, not a cat.
12. **Ears in the frame:** a tile shaped like a cat's head. **Risk:** gimmick.
